# Minor-Projects
A collection of small projects. Most of them were created as school projects between 2023 and 2025.

---
 
## Projects
 
### 🗺️ [Map Maker](./Map%20Maker)
A tile-based map editor. Paint sprites onto a grid, copy/paste regions, and save/load maps as JSON. Built with TypeScript and HTML Canvas.
 
### 🧭 [Path Finder](./Path%20Finder)
An interactive shortest path visualizer. Place a start, an end and obstacles on a 9×9 grid, then watch BFS find the shortest route.
 
### 🚢 [BattleShip Game](./BattleShip%20Game)
Classic Battleship against a bot. Place your fleet, then take turns firing at each other's hidden ships on a 10×10 grid.
 
### 🃏 [Memory Match Game](./Memory%20Match%20Game)
A card-matching memory game with three time modes (30/60/90s), a nickname system and a cookie-based leaderboard.
 
### 🐍 [Snake Game](./Snake%20Game)
Classic Snake on a 15x15 grid with sprite graphics — proper head, body, curve and tail segments that rotate with the snake's movement.
 
### 🏍️ [Speedway Game](./Speedway%20Game)
A local multiplayer top-down speedway racer for 2–4 players. Race on an oval dirt track, complete 3 laps and avoid crashing out.
 
### 🧵 [Dratewka](./Dratewka)
A text-based adventure inspired by the Polish folk tale of Skuba the Shoemaker. Explore a medieval map, collect items and craft a poisoned sheep to slay the dragon. *(Work in progress — final chapter not yet implemented)*
 
### 📂 [Archive](./Archive)
A directory of legacy, unfinished or experimental projects preserved for historical reference. Some may be incomplete, lack final assets or contain outdated configurations.
